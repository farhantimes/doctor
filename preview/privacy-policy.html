Patient Rights & Legal Standards

Privacy Policy & Confidentiality

How we protect your personal information, consultation records, and digital communications.

1. Commitment to Patient Confidentiality

At [CLINIC NAME], safeguarding your health information and personal identity is central to our clinical ethics. This policy explains how we collect, store, and process patient contact data and appointment requests through this website.

2. Information Collected via Website Forms

When you submit an appointment request on our website, we only collect the minimum information necessary to coordinate your booking:

  • Full Name
  • Contact Phone Number
  • Preferred Consulting Doctor or Department
  • Preferred Date and Time Window
  • Non-confidential administrative note

We do not collect sensitive clinical health histories, detailed diagnoses, or financial account credentials through basic public website forms. Comprehensive clinical records are gathered in-person during formal clinical consultations.

3. Use of Information

Your submitted contact information is used exclusively to:

  • Confirm, reschedule, or communicate regarding your requested consultation
  • Provide visit instructions and directions to our medical center
  • Comply with statutory clinical record-keeping obligations

We never sell, rent, or lease patient contact details to commercial third parties or advertisers.
